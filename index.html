I'm a computational scientist at Leidos with a Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Pittsburgh, and an R&D 100 Award recipient (2024) for pioneering work in ultrasonic photonics and distributed fiber-optic sensing. I build guided-wave NDT systems with fiber sensors (FBG, SMS, distributed acoustic) for structural health monitoring, and develop tensor-based signal processing and machine-learning methods that improve sensitivity and defect characterization.

I'm seeking challenging multidisciplinary projects and collaborations that advance next-generation sensing and monitoring technologies.

Selected Publications

Quasi-Distributed Fiber Sensor-Based Approach for Pipeline Health Monitoring: Generating and Analyzing Physics-Based Simulation Datasets for Classification
Pengdi Zhang, Abhishek Venketeswaran, Ruishu F. Wright, Nageswara Lalam, Enrico Sarcinelli, Paul R. Ohodnicki
Sensors, 2023

A framework for pipeline damage detection that converts simulated ultrasonic guided-wave data into distributed and quasi-distributed acoustic sensing responses, producing robust datasets for classifying pipeline events under varying noise conditions.

Deep Neural Network Assisted Distributed Strain and Temperature Fiber Sensor System for Natural Gas Pipeline Monitoring
Nageswara Lalam, Sandeep Bukka, Pengdi Zhang
IEEE Xplore, 2024

A natural gas pipeline integrity monitoring system using double-Brillouin-peak sensing fiber with BOTDA, demonstrating distributed temperature and pressure measurements in lab and pilot-scale tests, with a probabilistic deep neural network for rapid analysis validated against FEM models.

Ultrasonic Wave Propagation Simulation Tool Development
Pengdi Zhang, Abhishek Venketeswaran

Novel tensor decomposition algorithms that significantly improve sensitivity and noise rejection in distributed fiber-optic sensing systems for structural health monitoring.
